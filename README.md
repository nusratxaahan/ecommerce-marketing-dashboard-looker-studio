# 📊 Ecommerce Marketing Performance Dashboard (Looker Studio)

A 15-page Looker Studio dashboard for a jewelry ecommerce store. It combines Shopify, GA4, Meta Ads, Google Ads and Klaviyo data to track blended marketing efficiency (MER), ad performance and sales in one live report.

**Built by [Info Logic Solution](http://www.infologicsolution.com)**

## 🎯 Problem

Ad platforms report their own conversions and ROAS, which rarely match actual store revenue. Teams waste time switching between Shopify, GA4, Meta Ads, Google Ads and Klaviyo to answer one question: *is our ad spend profitable?*

## ✅ Solution

One dashboard that joins all sources, updates automatically and tracks MER (total sales ÷ total ad spend) alongside platform-level metrics.

## 📑 Dashboard Pages

| Section | Pages | What it shows |
|---------|-------|---------------|
| **Overall Performance** | Daily, Monthly | Sales, orders, AOV, ad spend, MER, CPC, conversion rate |
| **Country Performance** | Overall, Detailed | Sales, ad spend, MER, CPO and conversion rate by country |
| **Trends** | Session/Click, CPC/CTR | Daily spend, clicks, gross vs. net sales, CPC and CTR for Google and Meta |
| **Facebook Ads** | 1 page | Reach, CPM, CTR, purchases, ad set and campaign performance, spend by age, device and region |
| **Google Ads** | 1 page | Campaigns, ad groups, search terms, impression share, device and location breakdown |
| **Shopify Store** | 1 page | Total and gross sales, discounts, returns, product and SKU performance, orders by city |
| **Klaviyo** | 1 page | Email campaign performance, orders and conversions |
| **Google Analytics** | 4 pages | Users, channels, landing pages, product funnel, source/medium, first-user campaigns |

## 🔌 Data Sources

Shopify · Google Analytics 4 · Meta Ads · Google Ads · Klaviyo

## 📏 Key Metrics

- **MER** (Marketing Efficiency Ratio)
- ROAS (Meta and Google)
- CPC, CTR, CPM, CPO
- AOV and conversion rate
- Revenue by channel, country and product


## 💡 Insights Enabled

- Blended MER vs. platform-reported ROAS
- Which countries and campaigns deserve more budget
- Best-selling products and SKUs
- Email campaign impact during sales periods like Black Friday
